# Campus Overview

ITS Engineering College is located in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

The campus has been developed to support engineering education through modern classrooms, laboratories, innovation spaces, library facilities, sports infrastructure, and student activity areas.

The institute aims to provide an environment that supports academics, research, innovation, professional development, and extracurricular activities.
