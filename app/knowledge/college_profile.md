# Introduction

ITS Engineering College is a private engineering institution located in Knowledge Park III, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The college is a constituent institution of ITS – The Education Group, an educational group with a history of more than three decades that operates multiple institutions across engineering, management, pharmacy, dentistry, physiotherapy, and other professional disciplines.

The institution focuses on engineering and management education with emphasis on academic excellence, practical learning, industry collaboration, innovation, entrepreneurship, research, and skill development.

The college was established in 2006 and is approved by AICTE while being affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.
