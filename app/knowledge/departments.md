# Overview

ITS Engineering College offers engineering education through specialized academic departments. Each department focuses on quality teaching, practical laboratory experience, industry interaction, research, innovation, and student development.

The departments follow the curriculum prescribed by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) while incorporating skill development, projects, internships, and industry exposure.
