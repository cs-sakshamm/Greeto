# Admission FAQs

## Q: How can I get admission to ITS Engineering College?

A: Admission is available through the applicable UPTAC/AKTU counselling process or, where permitted and subject to eligibility and seat availability, through the institute's admission process. Candidates should verify the latest admission procedure with the Admission Office.

---

## Q: Is JEE Main compulsory?

A: Admission requirements depend on the applicable admission process and AKTU guidelines for the current academic session. Please refer to the latest admission notification.

---

## Q: Can I get direct admission?

A: Eligible candidates may apply through the institute's admission process subject to seat availability and applicable regulations.

---

## Q: What is the minimum percentage required for B.Tech?

A: Eligibility is governed by AKTU norms. Candidates should refer to the latest eligibility criteria published by the institute.

---

## Q: Is lateral entry available?

A: Yes. Eligible diploma holders or other qualified candidates may apply for B.Tech Lateral Entry according to AKTU regulations.

---

## Q: Which documents are required?

A: Students generally require Class 10 and 12 certificates, marksheets, Aadhaar Card, passport-size photographs, transfer certificate, character certificate, migration certificate (if applicable), and other documents specified during admission.
