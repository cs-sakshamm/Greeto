# Purpose

# General Information

Fees at ITS Engineering College are determined according to the program, admission category, and applicable university or government guidelines.

Students are advised to verify the latest fee structure with the Admission Office before making any payment, as fees may be revised for new academic sessions.

The total amount payable by a student may include one or more of the following components:

- Tuition Fee
- University Fee
- Examination Fee
- Registration Fee
- Security Deposit (if applicable)
- Library Charges
- Laboratory Charges
- Development Fee

# Hostel Fee

Hostel accommodation is available for eligible students.

Hostel charges generally include:

- Accommodation
- Room Maintenance
- Electricity (subject to hostel policy)
- Basic Facilities

Mess charges may be separate depending on the hostel rules applicable during the admission session.

Students should contact the Hostel Office for the latest hostel fee structure.
