# Engineering Glossary

## AI
Artificial Intelligence is the field of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

## Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a branch of AI where computers learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed.

Keywords:
ML
Supervised Learning
Regression
Classification
