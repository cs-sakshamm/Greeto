# Hostel Facilities - ITS Engineering College

---

# Purpose

This document provides structured information regarding hostel facilities, accommodation, rules, safety, dining, amenities, and frequently asked questions for students seeking admission to ITS Engineering College.

This document is optimized for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) so that the AI Admission Receptionist can accurately answer hostel-related questions.

# Overview

ITS Engineering College provides hostel facilities for students who wish to reside on campus during their academic program.

Hostel accommodation is intended to provide a safe, comfortable, and academically supportive environment for students coming from different cities and states.

Students should contact the Hostel Office for the latest information regarding room availability, hostel fees, room allocation, and hostel rules.

---

# Hostel Availability

Hostel accommodation is generally available for:

- Boys
- Girls

Separate hostel facilities are maintained.

Room allocation is subject to availability and completion of admission formalities.

# Hostel Admission Process

Students generally need to:

1. Complete college admission.
2. Apply for hostel accommodation.
3. Submit required documents.
4. Pay hostel fees.
5. Receive room allocation.
6. Complete hostel registration.

Room allotment is subject to availability.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does the college provide hostel facilities?

Yes. Separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls, subject to room availability.

---

## Is hostel compulsory?

No.

Students may choose hostel accommodation based on their personal preference.

---

## Are boys and girls accommodated separately?

Yes.

Separate hostel facilities are maintained.

---

## Does the hostel provide food?

Hostel residents generally have access to mess facilities serving daily meals.

---

## Is Wi-Fi available?

Internet facilities may be available according to current hostel infrastructure and policies.

---

## Can parents visit the hostel?

Visitors may be permitted during designated visiting hours according to hostel regulations.

---

## How do I apply for a hostel?

After securing admission, students can apply for hostel accommodation through the Hostel Office. Room allocation depends on availability.

---

## Is hostel available for first-year students?

Yes, subject to availability and completion of admission procedures.

---

## Is hostel fee included in tuition fees?

No.

Hostel charges are generally separate from academic tuition fees.
