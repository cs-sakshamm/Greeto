# Purpose

This document provides structured information regarding placements, internships, corporate training, career development, recruitment process, and the Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) at ITS Engineering College.

It is optimized for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to enable the AI Admission Receptionist to answer placement-related questions accurately.

# Overview

ITS Engineering College has a dedicated Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) that supports students in career development, internships, placement preparation, industry interaction, and campus recruitment.

The CRC works throughout the academic year to prepare students for employment opportunities and higher education.

Students are encouraged to participate in training programs, technical workshops, aptitude preparation, communication skill development, mock interviews, resume building, and industry interactions.

---

The Corporate Resource Centre serves as the primary interface between students and recruiters.
