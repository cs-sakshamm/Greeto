# Scholarships and Financial Assistance - ITS Engineering College

---

# Purpose

This document provides information regarding scholarships, financial assistance, fee concessions, education loans, and government scholarship schemes available to students of ITS Engineering College.

This knowledge base is designed for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to help an AI Admission Receptionist answer scholarship-related questions accurately.

---

# Overview

ITS Engineering College encourages eligible students to apply for scholarships offered by:

- Government of India
- Government of Uttar Pradesh
- AICTE
- Various scholarship portals
- Other approved funding agencies

Scholarships are awarded according to the eligibility criteria prescribed by the respective authorities.

---

# Types of Scholarships

Students may be eligible under one or more of the following categories.

## Government Scholarships

These scholarships are offered through Central Government and State Government schemes.

Examples include:

- Post Matric Scholarship
- National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
- Uttar Pradesh Scholarship Scheme

Eligibility and application timelines are determined by the respective government authorities.

---

## Merit-Based Scholarships

Meritorious students with outstanding academic performance may become eligible for merit-based scholarships if announced by the institution or sponsoring organizations.

Eligibility varies according to the applicable scholarship program.

---

## Category-Based Scholarships

Students belonging to eligible categories may apply under government scholarship schemes.

Categories may include:

- SC
- ST
- OBC
- EWS

Applicants should possess valid supporting certificates issued by competent authorities.

---

## Minority Scholarships

Eligible students belonging to notified minority communities may apply through the appropriate government scholarship portals if such schemes are active.

---

## Scholarships for Students with Disabilities

Students with benchmark disabilities may be eligible under various state and central government scholarship programs.

Eligibility is governed by the concerned department.

---

## AICTE Scholarship Schemes

Students may apply for AICTE-supported scholarship schemes whenever applicable.

Examples include schemes for:

- Economically weaker students
- Girls
- Differently-abled students

Availability depends on current AICTE notifications.

---

# Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the specific scholarship.

Common requirements include:

- Admission in an approved program
- Academic performance
- Income criteria
- Category certificate
- Domicile requirements
- Attendance requirements
- Required documentation

---

# Income Certificate

Many scholarship schemes require a valid income certificate issued by the competent authority.

Students should ensure that the certificate remains valid during the application period.

---

# Documents Required

Students should generally keep the following documents ready.

- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Passbook
- Passport Size Photograph
- Income Certificate
- Domicile Certificate
- Category Certificate
- Previous Marksheet
- Admission Letter
- Fee Receipt
- Bonafide Certificate
- Student ID Card
- Mobile Number
- Email ID

Additional documents may be required depending on the scholarship scheme.

Scholarship applications generally undergo:

- Institute Verification

# Important Guidelines

Students should:

- Apply before the deadline.
- Ensure documents are correct.
- Keep bank account active.
- Use their own Aadhaar-linked account where required.
- Track application status regularly.
- Preserve acknowledgement receipts.

# Education Loans

Students who require financial assistance may approach scheduled banks for education loans.

Loan approval is governed by the lending institution.

Students should directly contact the bank for:

- Eligibility
- Interest rates
- Repayment terms
- Required documents

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does ITS Engineering College provide scholarships?

Eligible students can apply under applicable government and approved scholarship schemes. Scholarship availability depends on the scheme and eligibility criteria.

---

## How do I apply for a scholarship?

Students should apply through the relevant scholarship portal and complete institute verification along with required documentation.

---

## Can first-year students apply?

Yes, if they satisfy the eligibility requirements of the respective scholarship scheme.

---

## Is an income certificate required?

Many scholarship schemes require a valid income certificate.

---

## What if my application is rejected?

Students should verify the rejection reason, correct any deficiencies if permitted, and contact the scholarship authority or institute for guidance.

---

## Can I receive more than one scholarship?

This depends on the rules of the respective scholarship schemes. Some schemes do not allow multiple benefits simultaneously.

---

## Is Aadhaar mandatory?

Many government scholarship schemes require Aadhaar-based verification.

---

## How will I receive the scholarship amount?

Approved scholarship amounts are generally transferred directly to the student's registered bank account according to the scheme rules.
